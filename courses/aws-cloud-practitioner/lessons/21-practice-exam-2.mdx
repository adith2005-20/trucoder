      ## Practice Exam 2 — 65 questions · 90 minutes · passing score 700/1000
      
      This second full-length practice exam simulates the real CLF-C02 in format,
      difficulty, and domain mix — and avoids repeating the scenarios from
      Practice Exam 1. Sit it like the real thing: 90 minutes, no notes, then
      review every explanation.
      
      - **Question types.** Multiple choice questions have one correct answer and
        three distractors. Multiple response questions ask you to select two
        correct responses out of four — select exactly the number requested; the
        exam software does not check that you selected the right count.
      - **No penalty for guessing.** Wrong answers do not subtract points, but
        unanswered questions are counted as incorrect. Answer everything.
      - **Scoring.** Scores range from 100 to 1000 and the passing score is 700.
        About 15 of the 65 questions are unscored pilot questions that AWS uses to
        evaluate future exam content — you cannot tell them apart, so treat every
        question as scored.
      - **Flag and skip.** Budget about 80 seconds per question. When a question
        stalls you, flag it, move on, and return with the time you saved on the
        questions you knew instantly.
      - **Eliminate distractors.** If an option names a service you have never
        seen in this course, it is almost certainly a distractor. Focus on the
        services you know and their trigger keywords.
      - **Domain weighting.** Cloud Concepts 24%, Security & Compliance 15%,
        Technology 34%, Billing & Pricing 27% — the same split as the real exam.
