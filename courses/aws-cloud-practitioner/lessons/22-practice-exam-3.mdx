      ## Practice Exam 3 — 65 questions · 90 minutes · passing score 700/1000

      This full-length practice exam mirrors the real AWS Certified Cloud
      Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam, with all four domains in the official
      proportions. Work through all 65 questions before reading the
      explanations, then use every explanation to review the
      concept it tests.

      - **Question types.** You will see two types: **multiple choice** (one
        correct answer and three incorrect distractors) and **multiple response**
        (the question tells you how many responses to select — select exactly
        that many, because the exam software does not warn you if you pick the
        wrong number of answers).
      - **Answer every question.** Unanswered questions are marked incorrect,
        and there is no penalty for guessing — so never leave a question blank.
      - **Unscored questions.** 50 of the 65 questions count toward your score;
        15 are unscored and used by AWS to validate future exam questions. You
        will not know which is which, so answer all of them seriously.
      - **Time management.** 65 questions in 90 minutes gives you roughly 80
        seconds per question. Flag questions you are unsure about, skip them,
        and come back at the end — do not burn ten minutes on a single question.
      - **Use elimination.** Services you have never seen are almost always
        distractors, not answers. Eliminate the options you know are wrong,
        then match the keywords of the scenario to the best remaining service.
      - **Domain weighting.** The real exam weighs Cloud Concepts 24%, Security &
        Compliance 15%, Technology 34%, and Billing & Pricing 27%. This exam
        follows the same proportions.
