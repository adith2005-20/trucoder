      ## Practice Exam 5 — 65 questions · 90 minutes · passing score 700/1000
      
      This is the fifth and final full-length practice exam for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) certification. It contains **65 questions** in the exact style of the real exam — a mix of single-answer multiple choice (mcq) and multi-select (mscq) — covering all four exam domains in the official weighting: **Cloud Concepts 24% (~16 questions), Security & Compliance 15% (~10), Technology 34% (~22), and Billing & Pricing 27% (~17)**. Aim for 700 out of 1,000 to pass, and budget 90 minutes.
      
      Exam strategy — apply these on the real test too:
      
      - **Manage your time.** 65 questions in 90 minutes is about **83 seconds per question**. Check the clock every 10 questions; if you are stuck on one, do not burn the whole exam on it.
      - **Proceed by elimination.** Most CLF-C02 questions are high-level "pick-a-service" scenarios. Rule out the options you know are wrong, then choose the one that makes the most sense. If a solution seems feasible but highly complicated, it is probably wrong — think simple.
      - **Flag and skip.** You can flag questions to review later, and you are **not penalized for wrong answers** — but an unanswered question counts as incorrect. Always select something before moving on.
      - **Select exactly the number requested.** For "Select TWO" questions, the exam software does **not** check that you picked the right count. If the prompt says select two, tick exactly two boxes — one or three will not score.
      - **Know the domain traps.** Cloud Concepts questions test definitions (pay-as-you-go, elasticity vs scalability, shared responsibility). Technology questions are service-selection scenarios. Billing & Pricing questions test pricing models, Free Tier limits, and support plans. Security questions test "who manages what" under the shared responsibility model.
      
      :::tip **Exam tip:**
      Read the scenario's verb before picking a service: "transcribe" → Amazon Transcribe, "decouple" → SQS, "audit who did what" → CloudTrail, "monitor CPU" → CloudWatch, "recommendations" → Personalize. The answer keyword is almost always in the question.
      :::
